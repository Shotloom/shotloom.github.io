Why it's built this way

Automate first, refine second

First session same evening You keep the tool when the first multicam session works the same evening and the steps feel repeatable. Bootstrap with Docker, open the dashboard, visible progress — no endless tutorial. From "I'll try it someday" to "I already processed a real shoot."
Long sessions don't block your day The problem isn't session length — it's being glued to the screen. Start ingest, move on to other work, come back to rough cut and exports. For Elena and Marco that's the difference between a one-off test and a weekly habit.
Privacy and cost stay predictable You move faster when you don't have to estimate monthly cloud NLE costs or wonder where rushes end up. Local Docker by default. Early access on GitHub while license terms are finalized.
