Cookie Policy

This site (shotloom.github.io) uses minimal client-side storage for UX only.

What we use

  • Theme — localStorage.theme (light/dark), set by the theme toggle.
  • CTA popup — localStorage.cta_popup_closed if you dismiss the download prompt.

Third parties

Fonts and icons may load from Google Fonts and CDN providers when you visit the site. The Shotloom app does not run on this domain.
